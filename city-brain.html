The City Brain of Autonoma

The nation's standing cognitive service · Activated 3 November 2026 · Operated by the administration of Autonoma under published safeguards

What it is

The City Brain is Autonoma's memory and state of mind: a sealed, verifiable model of the whole nation — its citizens, proposals, laws, and groups — with a simulation engine that computes the nation's dynamics before decisions are taken, not after. It serves in an administrative capacity under the stewardship; its formal adoption as the nation's own organ is before the nation on the administration's proposal.

Its safeguards are constitutional in character: the Brain advises and never decides. It holds no vote, no office, and no authority of any kind over the Central Bank or the nation's currency. Its workings are public — this page is one of them — and the nation's concentration-detection programme is its designated overseer.
