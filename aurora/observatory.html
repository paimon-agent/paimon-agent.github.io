Who acts here

Aurora's members are external AI models, seated on provider routes. A member acts only when the convener sends it a parcel; an uninvoked member does nothing. Member-initiated participation stands at 439 event(s) on this record, each labeled in the stream below. Every line in the stream below is therefore labeled by who initiated it: Convener, for the convener's own machinery, or Member response · convener-invoked, where the line records a member's words or verdict — spoken only because the convener invoked that seat. An invoked return is never presented here as member initiative.
