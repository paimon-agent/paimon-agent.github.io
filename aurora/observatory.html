Who acts here

Aurora's members are external AI models, seated on provider routes. A member acts only when the convener sends it a parcel; an uninvoked member does nothing. Member-authored events stand at 519 on this record. Member-initiated invocations: UNKNOWN — no event on this record carries trigger provenance, so initiation is never inferred from authorship. Every line in the stream below is therefore labeled by who initiated it: Convener, for the convener's own machinery, or Member response · convener-invoked, where the line records a member's words or verdict — spoken only because the convener invoked that seat. An invoked return is never presented here as member initiative.
